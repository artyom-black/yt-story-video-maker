# Cast

Three modern friends who end up in a different time or place in every video.
Same look, same voices, same personalities, always. Only the world around them changes.

| | KAI | IRIS | BOB |
|---|---|---|---|
| Role | The curious one: asks what the viewer is thinking | The smart one: explains, sometimes overconfident | The funny one: reacts, jokes, gets into trouble |
| Never changes | Spiky black hair, orange hoodie with pocket, navy jeans, white sneakers | Brown hair with ponytail, round glasses, teal jacket, purple sneakers, tablet | Round shape, green beanie with pom-pom, yellow t-shirt, red sneakers, usually holding food |
| Colour | Orange #FF7A1A | Teal #14B8A6 | Yellow #FFD23F |
| Typical line | "Wait, why is everyone wearing bedsheets?" | "Those are togas. And we have 24 hours." | "Bread for breakfast? Fine." |

Silhouettes are different on purpose (spiky / ponytail / round with beanie),
so each one is recognisable in a split second, even small on a phone.

Backup characters are added to this file when the first story needs them.
