TITLE:
What Did Ancient Humans Do When They Got Sick?

DESCRIPTION:
No doctors. No pills. No hospitals. So what did ancient humans actually do when they got sick or hurt? Real Stone Age teeth, bones and frozen bodies show the answer: Neanderthals chewing natural painkillers, the first fillings made of tar, holes cut in skulls, and the oldest surgery ever found, 31,000 years ago. Everything in this video is real science.
