RULES (MrBeast):
1. Title first. Use the proven question style that gets clicks:
   "What Did [people] Actually Do All Day?", "How Did [people] Survive
   Without [thing]?", "Why You Wouldn't Survive a Day as [person]",
   "When Did [people] Start [habit]?" A slightly gross or taboo angle
   gets the most clicks (toilets, smells, food, pain, money, sleep).
2. The first 5 seconds deliver the promise of the title. No intro, no
   "hey guys". Scene 1 is the most shocking moment of the whole idea.
3. By scene 5, promise something at the end ("there's one thing almost
   everyone gets wrong about these people") and pay it off at the end.
4. Something new or surprising every 2-3 scenes. Stakes keep rising.
   Around 3 minutes and 6 minutes, a big twist or reveal.
5. Every fact must be real. Add SOURCES (books or trusted sites).
   Bust at least two famous myths.
6. End fast: the payoff, then one line teasing the next video, then stop.
7. About 85-95 scenes, 1,500-1,700 words in total (about 10 minutes).
   One or two sentences per scene, never more than 40 words.
8. Kai talks like a real person: short sentences, reactions, jokes.
9. Never state a strange fact without the WHY. If a viewer would ask "why?",
   Kai answers it right away, and every reason gets its own THEN picture.

   small electric heater, all night. And inside, wolves and thieves can't
   get it. It's worth more than everything I own."

   touching Kai's face or sitting on his shoulder, never two bodies tangled

   Kai in separate pictures instead.
