HOW MRBEAST WOULD WRITE IT:
1. Seconds 0-5: show exactly what the thumbnail shows, and say the title's question out loud.
   No intro, no "hey guys". The viewer must feel "yes, this is what I clicked for".
2. By about 0:40: promise something for the end ("before this day ends, I'll show you the one
   thing almost everyone gets wrong about ..."), and pay it off at the very end.
3. Give Kai a GOAL with a CLOCK and real stakes (e.g. "get the wheat in before the storm, or my
   family starves"). Show the clock on screen (TEXT: 6 AM, 9 AM ...). It never goes backwards.
4. Something new, surprising, funny or gross every 20-30 seconds. Stakes keep rising.
   A big twist around 3 minutes and another around 5-6 minutes.
5. Show things happening to Kai, don't list facts. Bad: "Peasants paid many taxes."
   Good: "And look who's in my field: the church's man, taking every tenth bundle."
6. One last obstacle right before the win (the cart gets stuck, the rain starts), then the win.
7. After the win, end fast: the promised payoff, one line back to the thumbnail and title, one line
   teasing the next video. No extra facts after the climax.
8. Every fact must be real, with SOURCES. Bust at least two famous myths. Never state a strange
   fact without the WHY right after it, and give the why its own picture.
9. Length: 8-10 minutes = 1,300-1,600 spoken words, 55-80 scenes. One to three short sentences
   per scene, never more than 40 words. Kai talks like a real person: short sentences, reactions.
10. Every line must still make sense: the same people, animals and places all the way through,
   no contradictions (if his wheat is not cut, it can't be "half cut" later).
