TITLE: What Did Medieval Peasants Actually Do All Day?
CHARACTER: program/characters/stick/video1_reference.png
CLOTHES: a simple patched brown wool tunic down to his knees with a rope belt, and simple flat medieval leather wrap shoes tied with thin straps (no boots, no heels, nothing modern)
THUMBNAIL: 4 AM?! | side view, England in the year 1300, before sunrise in a cold muddy field, a dark blue sky full of stars and a thin orange line of dawn over distant hills and a small thatched village. On the right a big brown ox walks to the right, pulling a simple wooden medieval plough by a wooden yoke on its neck. Behind the plough, on the left, he walks after it, bent forward, both hands firmly on the two wooden plough handles that rise from the plough to his waist, the plough blade cutting a dark furrow in the mud. His face is exactly like image 0 (small eyes with tiny pupils), exhausted and miserable, sweat drops. Everything looks medieval, nothing modern
